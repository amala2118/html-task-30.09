GLOBAL WARMING

Global warming is the gradual increase in the Earth's average temperature. It is mainly caused by the release of greenhouse gases into the atmosphere through human activities. Burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial pollution are some of its major causes. Global warming can lead to melting glaciers, rising sea levels, extreme weather, and damage to wildlife. It can also affect agriculture, water resources, and human health. We can help reduce global warming by planting trees, saving electricity, using public transport, and reducing waste. Everyone has a responsibility to protect the environment and keep our planet safe for future generations.

EXPLANATION OF 10 HTML TAGS

1. <html> - Defines the HTML document.

2. <head> - Contains information about the webpage.

3. <title> - Defines the title shown in the browser tab.

4. <body> - Contains the visible content of the webpage.

5. <h1> - Defines the main heading.

6. <h2> - Defines a second-level heading.

7. <p> - Defines a paragraph of text.

8. <ul> - Creates an unordered bullet list.

9. <ol> - Creates an ordered numbered list.

10. <li> - Defines an item in a list.
